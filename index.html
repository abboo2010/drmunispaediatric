Our Services

Services That We Provide

Five areas of paediatric surgical care, from newborn surgery to keyhole procedures, for every stage of childhood.

General Paediatric Surgery

Everyday surgical care for children, from appendicitis and hernias to burns and tongue tie.

  • Appendicitis
  • Inguinal hernia
  • Tongue tie
Explore

Neonatal Surgery

Specialist surgery for newborns with conditions they are born with.

  • Pyloric stenosis
  • Hirschsprung's disease
  • Anorectal malformation
Explore

Paediatric Urology

Expert care for the kidneys, bladder and genital conditions in children.

  • Hypospadias
  • Hydronephrosis
  • Phimosis
Explore

Paediatric Laparoscopy

Keyhole surgery through tiny incisions for less pain and a faster recovery.

  • Appendicectomy
  • Hernia repair
  • Orchidopexy
Explore

Paediatric Gynaecological Surgery

Gentle surgical care for girls, from ovarian conditions to congenital anomalies.

  • Ovarian pathology
  • Labial adhesions
  • Congenital anomalies
