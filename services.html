Services & Conditions

Surgical care for every stage of childhood

From common day-case procedures to more complex conditions, every plan is built around what's gentlest for your child.

Our clinical focus

Minimally Invasive (Laparoscopic) Surgery

Wherever clinically appropriate, Dr. Muniswaran uses keyhole techniques to operate through very small incisions. This typically means less post-operative pain, a shorter hospital stay, and a faster return to normal activity for your child.

Laparoscopic appendicectomy Laparoscopic hernia repair Keyhole abdominal surgery
Most common

General Paediatric Surgery

Surgical treatment for the everyday conditions parents bring their children in for — most of which are day-case procedures with a quick recovery.

Hernias Hydroceles Undescended testis Circumcision Umbilical hernia
