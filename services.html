Our clinical focus

Minimally Invasive (Laparoscopic) Surgery

Wherever clinically appropriate, Dr. Muniswaran uses keyhole techniques to operate through very small incisions. This typically means less post-operative pain, a shorter hospital stay, and a faster return to normal activity for your child.

  • Laparoscopic appendicectomy
  • Laparoscopic hernia repair
  • Keyhole abdominal surgery

Everyday and specialist care

Gentle, age-appropriate surgical care for your child, from the most common day-case procedures to newborn surgery.

Most common

General Paediatric Surgery

Surgical treatment for the everyday conditions parents bring their children in for — most of which are day-case procedures with a quick recovery.

  • Hernias
  • Hydroceles
  • Undescended testis
  • Circumcision
  • Umbilical hernia
Newborn care

Newborn & Neonatal Surgery

Specialist surgical care for infants, including congenital conditions that require early — sometimes urgent — intervention shortly after birth.

  • Congenital conditions
  • Neonatal surgical emergencies
  • Early intervention
All ages

  • Infants
  • Young children
  • Adolescents
