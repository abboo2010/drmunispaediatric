General Paediatric Surgery

Common surgical conditions in children

Conditions
  • Appendicitis
  • Inguinal hernia
  • Undescended testis & torsion of the testis
  • Repair of lacerations
  • Burn injuries
  • Hydrocoele
  • Tongue tie
  • Lumps and bumps

Neonatal Surgery

Surgery in the newborn period

Conditions
  • Tracheo-esophageal fistula
  • Diaphragmatic hernia
  • Duodenal and other intestinal atresia
  • Anorectal malformation
  • Hirschsprung's disease
  • Malrotation
  • Pyloric stenosis
  • Congenital abdominal wall defects (gastroschisis / omphalocele)

Paediatric Urology

Disorders of the urinary tract and genitalia

  • Disorders of the kidneysCysts and tumours of the kidney, infections such as renal abscess, hydronephrosis, etc.
  • Disorders of the ureterPelviureteric junction obstruction, vesicoureteric junction obstruction, vesicoureteral reflux, etc.
  • Disorders of the bladderNeurogenic bladder, bladder exstrophy, etc.
  • Disorders of the urethraPhimosis, posterior urethral valve, hypospadias, epispadias, etc.
  • Disorders of the genitaliaUndescended testis, intersex conditions, imperforate hymen.
  • Tumours of the kidneyWilms' tumour, etc.
