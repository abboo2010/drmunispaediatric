A specialist approach, built on gentleness

Dr. Muniswaran A/L Nadesin Consultant Paediatric Surgeon

Dr. Muniswaran A/L Nadesin is a Consultant Paediatric Surgeon based in Kota Kinabalu, Sabah. He holds an M.D. from Universiti Sains Malaysia (USM) and a Master of Medicine (M.Med.) in Paediatric Surgery from Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM), and is a registered Paediatric Surgery specialist with the Malaysian Medical Council.

His practice places a strong emphasis on minimally invasive (laparoscopic) techniques — an approach chosen to reduce pain, minimise surgical trauma, and help children return to their normal lives faster. Dr. Muniswaran treats a broad range of surgical conditions across infancy, childhood and adolescence, and holds surgical privileges at Gleneagles Hospital Kota Kinabalu for procedures that require hospital-based facilities.

Beyond clinical practice, he is an active contributor to Malaysia's paediatric surgery community, having spoken at the MAPS (Malaysian Association of Paediatric Surgery) Child Health Symposium on minimally invasive paediatric surgery.
